About Fresh

Our story

Fresh started small — a screen-printing side hustle in 2021, selling hand-printed tees at local pop-up markets and taking orders over DM. The black-and-yellow look caught on faster than expected, especially with students and creatives, and pretty soon the pop-ups and DMs couldn't keep up with demand.

Now it's a proper online brand, moving past just t-shirts into a wider streetwear and accessories range — while keeping the same attitude that got it here. Style for all, no overthinking required.
